Secret Jems

Our December gift swap. Add your wish list, spin the wheel, and find out who you’re spoiling this holiday season.

How the game works

  1. Find your name. Pick your office, choose your name from the staff list and create a 4-digit PIN.
  2. Make your wish list. Add three gift ideas, favourite first. Come back with your PIN to change them until 13 November.
  3. Wait for the draw. It opens for everyone on 16 November.
  4. Spin the wheel. It lands on the colleague you’re buying for and shows you their wish list.
  5. Shop and keep it secret. Stay within the spend limit and bring your wrapped gift on swap day.

Nobody can draw their own name, and only you can see who you got. Not even the organiser knows the pairs.
